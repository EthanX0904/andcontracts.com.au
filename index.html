About AND CONTRACTS

AND CONTRACTS PTY LTD is an Australian-owned contract logistics provider specialising in integrated supply chain solutions. We combine warehousing, distribution, and transportation services with a strong focus on bulk cargo and import/export logistics.

Operating from our network across Australia, including the Riverina Intermodal Freight and Logistics (RiFL) hub in Wagga Wagga, we provide cost-effective, reliable logistics solutions tailored to your business needs.

  • Australian-owned and operated
  • Specialists in bulk cargo and international trade
  • Integrated road-rail network across eastern seaboard
  • Advanced IT and supply chain management systems
  • Sustainability-focused operations

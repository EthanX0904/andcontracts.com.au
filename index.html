About

Contract logistics,
built around you

AND CONTRACTS PTY LTD is an Australian-owned contract logistics provider. We combine warehousing, distribution and transportation with a focus on bulk cargo and international trade.

From our network across Australia — including the Riverina Intermodal Freight & Logistics (RiFL) hub in Wagga Wagga — we deliver cost-effective supply chain solutions.
